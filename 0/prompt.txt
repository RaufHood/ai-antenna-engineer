🚀 Challenge Selection & Submission
Challenge selection is done by your team's captain at ehl.gg/matches/munich-2.
Challenge Selection
Once all team members have checked in, choose your challenge before the deadline. You can change your selection as often as you like until the deadline closes.

Repo & Tooling
Create your team's repository and set up Entire. On your repository's main branch, run:
curl -fsSL https://entire.io/install.sh | bash
Then run entire enable. Your agent will confirm whether it has been linked successfully.



On your next commit, link the agent's session context to the commit. You'll see the confirmation [entire] Pushing 1 checkpoint ref(s) to origin.... done. Then you're all set ✅
Challenge Submission
You can submit as often as you want before the submission deadline. Make sure you upload all relevant content and use Entire; otherwise your submission won't be accepted.

Judging runs in two rounds:
Judging runs in two rounds. In the first round, teams are judged across three rooms per challenge. The top two teams per track then advance to the final round, where they pitch again on the main stage to determine the overall winner. Note that finalists are not announced in advance but they are called to the stage one by one.

🆘 FAQ
Can I join without a team?
Yes. Team formation will happen during the event.
Can I sleep at the venue?
Yes. You can stay overnight at the venue. But just a heads-up: there are no beds or couches, so bring a sleeping bag and mat if you want to catch some sleep.
Do I need to bring my own laptop?
Yes.
